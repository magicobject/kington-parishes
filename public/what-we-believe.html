    The Christian faith

    What we believe

    What is the Christian faith, and why do we think it matters? Here is a short, plain-English answer — and some ways to dig deeper for yourself.

    The heart of it

    Christians believe there is one God, who made everything and loves what he has made. We believe that God did not stay distant: in Jesus of Nazareth, who lived some 2,000 years ago, God came among us to show us what he is like, to forgive us, and to give us a new way to live.

    Jesus taught about God's kingdom, healed the sick and welcomed those whom others ignored. He was put to death on a cross, and Christians believe that God raised him to life again on the third day. That is the good news at the centre of our faith: that death and failure are not the end, and that a fresh start is offered to everyone.

    Jesus went to the cross willingly, for us. He took on himself the weight of our wrongdoing so that we could be forgiven and made right with God. Forgiveness is at the very heart of Christianity. And being forgiven frees us from more than guilt: knowing how much we have been forgiven helps us to let go of the burden of holding grudges, and to forgive other people in turn.

    Christians also believe that God is with us now, by his Holy Spirit, who helps us to pray, to love our neighbours and to follow Jesus day by day. We call God Father, Son and Holy Spirit — one God, in three persons.

    Why we think it matters

    If this is true, it changes everything. It means we are known and loved, that our lives have meaning and purpose, and that forgiveness, hope and peace are real. It gives us a reason to care for one another and for the world — which is why our five churches are so involved in village life, from the foodbank to the Parish Hall.

    You don't need to have it all worked out to be welcome here. Questions, doubts and curiosity are part of every journey of faith.

    Part of the Church of England

    Our five churches are part of the Church of England, which is in turn part of the worldwide Christian family. You can read more about what the Church of England believes on its own website.

    Dig deeper

    If you'd like to explore further, these are good places to start:

    • The Chosen — a TV series about the life of Jesus, seen through the eyes of those who met him.
    • The Jesus Film — a 127-minute film of the story of Jesus, free to watch online.
    • All About Jesus — answers to common questions about who Jesus is.
    • Our own Resources page has books, prayer and courses.

    Or just come along to a service, or get in touch — we'd love to chat.
