Walking

Walkers welcome

Kington sits on the Offa's Dyke Path, and our own Pilgrim Paths lead out from each of our five churches into the hills and valleys around them. Wherever your walk takes you, you're always welcome to stop in.

Come in for a cup of tea

St Mary's, Kington is open during the day, and walkers are very welcome to come in, make themselves a cup of tea, enjoy some biscuits, and use the facilities. Whether you're partway round a Pilgrim Path or passing through on Offa's Dyke, stop, rest, and take a moment in a building that's offered shelter to travellers for centuries.

Walks from our churches

Our four Pilgrim Paths are way-marked circular walks through the hills and valleys of the border country, each starting and finishing at one of our churches — download a leaflet before you set off.

Kington also sits directly on the Offa's Dyke Path, the 177-mile National Trail following the England/Wales border from Chepstow to Prestatyn.
