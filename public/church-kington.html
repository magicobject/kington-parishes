    St Mary the Virgin, Kington

    Sunday worship, choir, and a livestreamed service each week, in the heart of the town.

    A regular act of worship every Sunday at 10:00am — normally a Common Worship Order One Service of Holy Communion. We're fortunate to have the services of our organist and a talented choir; the aim behind all our worship is to offer the best we can in thanksgiving, welcoming and engaging while retaining a real sense of awe.

    Sundays, 10:00am — Holy Communion
