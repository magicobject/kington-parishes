    Vacancies

    Current vacancies

    Two ongoing ways to help, right now — whether that's an hour or two on a Friday, or taking on a role for the longer term.

    Treasurer

    St Mary's, Kington is looking for a new Treasurer — a volunteer role working as part of the St Mary's, Kington PCC. No previous treasury experience is essential; full support and a proper handover will be given. There's more about the day-to-day of the role on the Blog, and a full Treasurer job description →.

    If you'd like to find out more, or think this could be for you, please get in touch with Greg Wright.

    Kington Foodbank volunteers

    Kington Foodbank is looking for volunteers to help for a few hours on a Friday, when it's open. It's run by Churches Together in Kington and based at the Parish Hall — anyone can help, though because volunteers may have contact with vulnerable adults, a DBS check has to be carried out first; we'll help arrange this once you get in touch.

    Visit the Kington Foodbank website →

    If you'd like to find out more, or think this could be for you, please get in touch with Phillippa Wright.
