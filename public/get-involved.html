Support, hire, and take part

Our mission — to create places of worship relevant to life in 21st century England, however quiet the setting — depends on this community's time, hall bookings, and fundraising.

Hire the Parish Hall

Our parish hall, next to St Mary's, is available for hire, with plenty of parking to the rear of the building and outside the church.

Contact Penny Halcrow, booking secretary →

The Church Fete

Held annually in July, our church fete is always a joyous occasion — stalls, games, competitions, music, and scrumptious teas — and raises much-needed funds for church upkeep.

Fundraising

From flower festivals to concerts, one-off fundraising events bring the parish together while supporting the upkeep of these historic buildings. Get in touch if you'd like to organise or support one.
