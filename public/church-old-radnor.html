    St Stephen's, Old Radnor

    Home to the UK's oldest free-standing organ case, beneath the hills of Radnor Forest.

    Worship follows Common Worship Order One for Communion Services. We're privileged to possess an extremely fine organ, with the oldest free-standing organ case in the UK. Ruth, our talented organist (shared with Kinnerton), leads the singing. On the third Sunday, our evening worship — "Junior Praise" — is less formal and designed to be relevant to the young and young at heart.
