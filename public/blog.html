    We're recruiting a Treasurer for St Mary's, Kington

    2 September 2026 · Greg Wright

    St Mary's, Kington is actively recruiting a new Treasurer. The role keeps the church's finances in good order — you'll be working closely with our bookkeeper, who handles the day-to-day spreadsheet, while reporting to the PCC and working alongside our churchwardens and clergy. No previous treasury experience is essential; full support and a proper handover will be given.

    If you'd like to find out more, or think this could be for you, please get in touch with Greg Wright.
