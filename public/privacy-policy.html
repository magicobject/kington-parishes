    Privacy Policy

    How we handle the personal details you give us when you sign up for the InSpire Newsletter.

    What we collect

    When you sign up for the InSpire Newsletter, we collect your email address, and your first name if you choose to give it.

    Why we collect it

    Only to send you the newsletter itself. You're added to our mailing list once you click the confirmation link we send you — nothing is sent to you before then.

    Who we share it with

    Your details are stored with MailerLite, the email service we use to send the newsletter. We don't share your details with anyone else, and we don't use them for anything beyond sending this newsletter.

    How long we keep it

    For as long as you stay subscribed. Every issue includes an unsubscribe link — using it removes you from the list straight away.

    Your rights

    You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any issue, or by contacting us directly and asking us to remove you. You can also ask what details we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete them.

    Contact us

    Questions about this policy, or about your details, can go to vicar@kingtonparishes.org.uk.
