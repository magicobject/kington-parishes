    Worship, week by week

    Prayer and outreach lie at the heart of what we do — welcoming and inclusive worship, with room for everyone to find their own way to the divine.

    How we worship together

    Regular & occasional services

    Group Eucharist

    On the fifth Sunday of the month, all five parishes join together as one family for a Group Eucharist at 10:00am, rotating around each church in turn.

    Choral Evensong

    That same evening, we gather in a different church for Choral Evensong, with our Benefice Choir leading the singing.

    Livestreamed services

    Can't join us in person? Our services are streamed live so you can take part from home.
