    September 2026

    News from across the Kington Parishes this month.

    Celebrating 25 years of ministry

    This year, Revd Sally Welch celebrates 25 years of ordained ministry. It's a wonderful milestone, and one we're delighted to mark together as a benefice.

    Revd Phillippa Wright ordained priest

    On 4 July, Revd Phillippa Wright was ordained priest. Please keep her, and her ministry among us, in your prayers.

    Still looking for a Treasurer at St Mary's, Kington

    St Mary's, Kington is still looking for a permanent Treasurer. If you have a head for figures and some time to give, read more about the role and how to get in touch on our blog →

    September's Wild Church cancelled

    Unfortunately, September's Wild Church has had to be cancelled, as several of our regular volunteers are away on holiday. We're sorry for the short notice, and we look forward to seeing you at the next one.
