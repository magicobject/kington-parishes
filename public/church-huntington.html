    St Thomas à Becket, Huntington

    A Grade II* listed church three miles west of Kington, first recorded in the Domesday Book.

    A Grade II* listed building with ancient oak pews and stained glass by the acclaimed Henry Payne. Its organ was originally built by the Positive Organ Company of London, founded by Thomas Casson — a banker turned organ builder whose ideas were advanced for the time — and has recently undergone a substantial upgrade. Huntington is a predominantly farming community, three miles west of Kington on the Welsh border, first recorded in the Domesday Book of 1086.

    "We live in a peaceful part of the world, but entering the church takes you to a quieter place still."

    Holy Eucharist, every 2nd Sunday — Evensong, every 4th Sunday
    Silence & prayer, alternate Wednesdays
