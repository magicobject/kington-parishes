St Mary the Virgin, Kington

Sunday worship, a warm welcome, and a livestreamed service each week, near the heart of the town.

Join us for worship

Come as you are — you'll find a warm welcome every Sunday at 10:00am — normally a Common Worship Order One Service of Holy Communion, with a more informal Morning Praise service on the third Sunday of the month. On the first Sunday we also gather for Praise & Prayer in the Evening, a quieter service of worship and prayer at 6:00pm. On a fifth Sunday, join us and the other four parishes for a rotating Group Eucharist — see our calendar for which church is hosting that month. We're fortunate to have the services of our organist and a talented choir; the aim behind all our worship is to offer the best we can in thanksgiving, welcoming and engaging while retaining a real sense of awe.

Do stay afterwards for freshly brewed tea and coffee, with biscuits, squash and — very often — cake. Children are welcome to play in our play area throughout the service, so bring the whole family along.

History & architecture

The church — properly the Church of St Mary the Virgin, though "St Mary's" is the name everyone uses day to day — stands on rising ground above the town, close to the site of the former Kington Castle, founded in the reign of Henry I (1100–1135). A defensive tower was built here around 1200, originally freestanding; the nave and aisles followed around 1300, the chancel was rebuilt and a south porch added in the early 14th century, and a Decorated-style south chapel was added around 1320. The double-broach spire was rebuilt in 1794, and an outer north aisle was added around 1874.

Inside, a Norman circular font with rope moulding and an incised zig-zag pattern survives from the church's earliest phase, and the south chapel holds the alabaster tomb-chest of Sir Thomas Vaughan of Hergest Court and his wife Ellen Gethin. The church is listed Grade I (Historic England list entry 1208031, first listed 8 October 1953).

Accessibility

  • Disabled parking is close to the main entrance, right beside the base of the tower.
  • There's a moveable ramp to the right of the main entrance door.
  • All well-behaved dogs are welcome.
  • We have a hearing loop.
  • The main parking is on the north side. It's a grassed area and will be muddy in wet weather — there's additional parking behind the Parish Hall nearby.
  • Large print service sheets are available.
  • Toilets are located behind the curtain covering the entrance on the north side of the church, opposite the door you came in through.
