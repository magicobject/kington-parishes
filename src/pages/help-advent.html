Help / Advent calendar

The Advent calendar

A calendar of 25 doors for December, with one short Bible story behind each. It is being built in stages and is not public yet: nobody can find it from this website or from a search engine. This page explains how to look at it early, what you will see, how to change a day's words or picture, and how to switch it on for everyone.

Try it before December

How to look at it on any date

The calendar opens a new door each day, so before December there is nothing to see. To get round that, add a pretend date to the end of the web address.

1. Open the calendar page

Go to the website address followed by /advent. The page is not linked from anywhere, so type it in or bookmark it.

2. Add a pretend date

Put ?advent-preview=2026-12-07 straight after it, so the whole address ends /advent?advent-preview=2026-12-07. The date is year, month, day. That example pretends it is 7 December 2026.

3. Look for the yellow notice

A notice at the top says "Preview mode: not today's date" and shows the date being pretended. Click Exit preview in the notice to go back to the real date.

Good to know

Only dates from 1 November to the end of the following January work, and only on the calendar page. A date that does not work is quietly ignored. Nothing is saved on your device and nobody else sees the pretend date.

What to expect

What you will see on each date

Dates are always worked out in UK time, wherever the visitor is. At the moment the page shows plain placeholder doors, so you can check the dates are right; the real doors, pictures and stories come in a later stage.

1 to 30 November

A calm sentence, "The Advent calendar opens on 1 December.", and no doors.

1 to 25 December

Doors 1 to 25. Today's door is highlighted, earlier doors are marked "available" and later ones "not yet". For example, on 12 December doors 1 to 11 are available, door 12 is today and door 13 onwards are not yet.

26 December to 6 January

Every door is available and none is "today".

7 January to 31 October

The same calm sentence, "The Advent calendar opens on 1 December.", and no doors. (It reads the same before and after the season.)

Words and pictures

How to change a day's text or picture

As with any change to this website, just ask Claude in plain English. For example: "Change the prayer on day 12 to say this" or "Add the picture for day 5".

The words

All 25 days live in one file. Each has a title, a Bible reference, the story, a question to wonder about, something to do, a short prayer, a description of the picture and the name of the Bible translation. Claude checks every change against strict rules (no web addresses, no odd characters, sensible lengths) and the site will not build if a day breaks them.

The pictures

Each day has one picture, a simple SVG drawing named day-01.svg to day-25.svg. Claude checks each one is safe (no scripts, no links to anywhere else) before it is used. Until a picture exists, that day simply has none.

Nothing is automatic about the text

The words only change when you ask. The generated copies of each day are made by the website build and should never be edited by hand.

Switching it on

How to go live

Going live is a decision for the person in charge of the website, and it is more than flipping a switch: the page you see in a preview today shows plain placeholder doors, so the real calendar has to be built and checked first. There is a step-by-step launch checklist in the website's files, docs/advent/launch-checklist.md. Ask Claude to follow it with you.

The short version

Build and check the real doors, add and check all 25 pictures, switch on the "pictures required" and "public listing" settings, remove two safety-net tests that exist only to keep the calendar hidden, run every test, deploy, then check the live site.

Before you do

Look at every date using the preview trick above, read every day's words, and look at every picture.
