Help / Advent calendar

The Advent calendar

A calendar of 25 doors for December, with one short Bible story behind each. It is being built in stages and is not public yet: nobody can find it from this website or from a search engine. This page explains how to look at it early, what you will see, how to change a day's words or picture, and how to switch it on for everyone.

Try it before December

How to look at it on any date

The calendar opens a new door each day, so before December there is nothing to see. To get round that, add a pretend date to the end of the web address.

1. Open the calendar page

Go to the website address followed by /advent. The page is not linked from anywhere, so type it in or bookmark it.

2. Add a pretend date

Put ?advent-preview=2026-12-07 straight after it (or after the home page address, to see the banner), so the whole address ends /advent?advent-preview=2026-12-07. The date is year, month, day. That example pretends it is 7 December 2026.

3. Look for the yellow notice

A notice at the top says "Preview mode: not today's date" and shows the date being pretended. Click Exit preview in the notice to go back to the real date.

Good to know

Only dates from 1 November to the end of the following January work (before launch this is the only way to see the calendar at all, whatever today's date), and only on the calendar page and the home page. A date that does not work is quietly ignored. Nothing is saved on your device and nobody else sees the pretend date.

What to expect

What you will see on each date

Dates are always worked out in UK time, wherever the visitor is. The calendar is now the real thing: arched doors on a night sky with the five churches along the ridge, and a story panel behind each door.

1 to 30 November

A calm sentence, "The Advent calendar opens on 1 December.", and no doors.

1 to 25 December

Doors 1 to 25. Today's door is gold and says "Today", earlier doors are open and later ones have a dashed edge and their date. A line says "Today is Day 12" and its title, and a button "Open today's door" opens it. On 25 December the star appears over the ridge and the star door fills the last row. For example, on 12 December doors 1 to 11 are open, door 12 is today and door 13 onwards are not yet.

26 December to 6 January

The page is called "The Christmas Story". Every door is open to read (a quiet "Start with Day 1" button helps people begin), none is "today", and the star stays over the ridge. If the 6 January bonus door has its text, it opens on 6 January.

7 January to 31 October

The same calm sentence, "The Advent calendar opens on 1 December.", and no doors. (It reads the same before and after the season.)

Opening a day

Click a door and the story opens in a panel: the picture (once it exists), the Bible reference, the story, "Think about it" and a prayer. Buttons go to the day before and after, share or copy a link to that day, and print the day on its own page. Escape or Close puts you back on the calendar. If a day will not load you get a friendly message and a "Try again" button. The calendar notices by itself when midnight passes, so a page left open still shows the right door.

A door that is not open yet

A small card says "Not quite yet. This door opens on 15 December." with two buttons, "Open it anyway" and "Maybe later". Nothing is remembered about what anyone has opened.

The home page banner

While the calendar is open (1 December to 6 January) a banner under the top of the home page shows today's day and a button to the calendar. Before launch it only appears when you add ?advent-preview=2026-12-07 to the home page address; the real date shows nothing (and a preview on the home page shows a "Preview mode" notice too).

Words and pictures

How to change a day's text or picture

As with any change to this website, just ask Claude in plain English. For example: "Change the prayer on day 12 to say this" or "Add the picture for day 5".

The words

All 25 days live in one file. Each has a title, a Bible reference, the story, a question to wonder about, a short prayer, a description of the picture and the name of the Bible translation. Claude checks every change against strict rules (no web addresses, no odd characters, sensible lengths) and the site will not build if a day breaks them.

The pictures

Each day has one picture, a simple SVG drawing named day-01.svg to day-25.svg (and day-26.svg for the bonus door), put in public/advent/art. Claude checks each one is safe before it is used: only plain shapes, gradients and re-used figures are allowed; no text, scripts, links to anywhere else, or more than 50 repeats of a figure. It also checks a picture is cheap enough for a phone to draw: masks and clip paths are limited (at most 20 uses and 50 shapes inside one), a very detailed outline counts for more than a simple one, dashed lines are not allowed, and the whole picture has a work budget, so a picture that would make a browser slow is refused. Before launch every picture is also timed in a real browser. The pictures for all 26 days are in. To replace one, give Claude the new SVG and say which day: it is saved under the same name (for example day-07.svg), checked as above, and its description must match the day's picture description in the words file. A day whose picture file is missing simply has none, until launch, when the website refuses to build without all of them.

The 6 January bonus door

"Gifts for a King" (the wise travellers and their gifts, Matthew 2) is a 26th door labelled "Epiphany". It is locked until 6 January, when it opens like any other day. Its picture is day-26.svg, like the others.

Nothing is automatic about the text

The words only change when you ask. The generated copies of each day are made by the website build and should never be edited by hand.

Switching it on

How to go live

Going live is a decision for the person in charge of the website, and it is more than flipping a switch: the real calendar is built, but it still needs its pictures, the reviews and the checklist. There is a step-by-step launch checklist in the website's files, docs/advent/launch-checklist.md. Ask Claude to follow it with you.

The short version

Check the real doors (built), add and check all 25 pictures, then switch on three settings together, go live, public listing and pictures required (the website refuses to build if only some are on), remove the three safety-net tests that exist only to keep the calendar hidden, run every test, deploy, then check the live site.

Until we go live

The real date shows nothing: not even in December does the calendar page show more than the calm sentence, and the home page shows no banner. The only way to see the calendar is the pretend date in the address. The "go live" setting is what changes that, and it cannot be switched on without the other two.

Before you do

Look at every date using the preview trick above, read every day's words, and look at every picture.
