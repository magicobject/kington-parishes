Service Setup

Things you might need to know when setting up for a service at St Mary's, Kington. Adapted from a handover note, so some details (names, who usually does what) may change.

Church services: Communion

In the church

  • Put out the brass crosses and candlesticks on the altars. The wooden ones are usually put under or behind the altars for convenience.
  • Put the brass lectern on the main altar, on the celebrant's left hand side.
  • Two hymn books go on the altar, one each side, with a note of the last hymn number (a number board can't be seen from there).
  • Put service books, hymn books and pew sheets out for the administrators, with notes of the offertory hymn number as well, and take the hassocks off their seats.
  • The offertory table needs to be put at the crossing, opposite the visitors' bookshelf. If you move it a bit towards the door the floor is more even.
  • Service books go from the vestry to the table. The pew sheets (many are folded together) may have been separated on the Saturday; if not, that needs doing. You will find them on the metal cupboard outside the vestry.
  • Brenda always has a set of books put out where she sits: a service book, a large print hymn book and a large print pew sheet, beside the pillar on the pew behind Ann.

In the vestry

  • It's useful to at least partly put out the communion things onto the counter in the vestry, particularly if the celebrant is going to be short of time getting from the villages.
  • The round silver tray is in the right hand vestry cupboard, in a green baize bag. The wafers are in a brown cardboard box in the same cupboard, along with the cruets (the stoppers are in with the wafers). Communion wine is at the bottom.
  • The altar linen and the associated green textiles are in the left hand chest of drawers under the counter, in the second drawer down.
  • Phillippa can tell you where it all goes, and has a very useful set of pictures. Ask a churchwarden for where the keys to the register and silver plate cupboard are kept.

Sound system

  • There is an instruction sheet on top of the sound system cabinet in the vestry.
  • Lectern and lapel mics are used for communion services.
  • 3rd Sunday: use the lectern mic, and for the other side desk, the microphone from the pulpit. This is posted through the side of the pulpit, and fits into one of the stands in the corner of the vestry opposite the door.

Hymn boards

Usually done by Dennis Forder. There are lists of the hymns in the clergy vestry and in the choir vestry.

Heaters

There is an instruction sheet in the vestry, and one attached to the control panel, which is behind the right hand side red curtain behind the Barker altar.

Live streaming

Usually set up by Paul. There is an instruction sheet in the vestry.

Urns

A good idea to put these on by at least 9.15, so that they can be turned down before the service, which makes it less likely that the smoke alarm goes off. The water heater also needs switching on (the switch is on the wall opposite the kitchen door).

Rota for readers, etc

There is a copy on the noticeboard in the vestry, and the current rotas are on the Rotas page.

SumUp

SumUp is kept in the sound system cabinet, with instructions.
