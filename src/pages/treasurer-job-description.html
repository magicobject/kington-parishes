Treasurer role description

What the role involves, day to day and across the year, for anyone thinking about stepping in as our next Treasurer at St Mary's, Kington.

The role

What the Treasurer looks after

You won't be doing this alone — our bookkeeper handles the day-to-day spreadsheet, and the wider PCC and clergy team are there to help.

Record keeping

Working with our bookkeeper to keep the accounts accurate and up to date, checking that income and payments are allocated to the right categories, and keeping invoices on file for the independent examiner.

Income

Recording cash, cheque and bank transfer receipts, claiming Gift Aid, and raising invoices for Parish Hall hire, weddings and funerals, and shared costs with the other parishes in the benefice.

Expenditure

Paying invoices as they arrive, keeping an eye on utility bills, and making the regular payments to the Diocese for parish share and statutory fees.

Transfers & investments

Keeping track of current and deposit account balances and cash flow, and overseeing our modest investment holdings.

Budgeting

Publishing regular progress reports against the year's budget, using our existing spreadsheet tools.

Reporting

Answering the PCC's questions on income and spending, preparing the end-of-year accounts for independent examination, and completing the annual returns to the Diocese and the Charity Commission.

Across the year

How the work is spread out

None of it needs doing all at once — most of it falls into a predictable rhythm.

As and when

Paying bills as they come in, recording invoices, reimbursing out-of-pocket expenses, and keeping bank signatories up to date as the PCC changes.

Weekly

A short reconciliation of recent transactions with our bookkeeper.

Monthly

A financial report to the PCC, plus paying clergy and staff-related costs.

Quarterly

Invoicing the other parishes in the benefice for their share of shared costs.

Annually

Renewing insurance, reviewing our energy contracts, preparing the annual accounts, and completing the statutory returns to the Diocese and the Charity Commission.
